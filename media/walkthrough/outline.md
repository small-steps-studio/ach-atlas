## Outline and hover

The **ACH Atlas** view in the activity bar lists the file header, every batch with its SEC code, company, entry count and totals, the entries with name and amount, their addenda, and the controls. Click a line to jump to the record. Large batches are listed in pages of 500 entries.

Rest the pointer on any field of a record. The hover shows:

* the record type and the field name,
* the positions (for example 30-39) and the raw characters,
* what the value means: the amount with its cents, the date with its weekday, the routing number with the result of its check digit, the transaction code as account type and direction,
* for control fields, the value computed from the entries.

The editor shows a ruler at column 94 and keeps the trailing spaces of ACH files when you save.
