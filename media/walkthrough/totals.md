## Totals and navigation

The status bar shows two things for the active ACH file:

* the field at the cursor, for example `Batch 1 > Entry 2 > Amount (30-39)`; click it to copy the path,
* the batches, entries, debits and credits of the file, computed from the entries, with the number of problems; click it for **Show File Totals**.

**Go To Batch** lists every batch with its company, SEC code, effective date and totals.
